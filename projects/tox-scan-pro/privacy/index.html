TOX SCAN PRO / PRIVACY

Privacy Policy

Draft — not approved for publication

This is an implementation-based draft reviewed on 14 September 2026, not an effective published policy. The owner must confirm the items below and the behavior of the released app before publication or App Store submission.

Data used by the app

Firebase Authentication provides an account identifier. Profile data may include a name, age, gender, height, weight, goals, allergies, sensitivities, avoided ingredients, food preferences, language, and family-member profiles. Firestore stores profiles, saved scan reports, favorites, and account activity. The app also records terms acceptance, scan allowances, subscription and purchase information, referral activity, and recovery information.

The iPhone stores authentication and recovery credentials using secure device storage. Camera and photo-library access supply images for scans; image processing creates temporary device cache files.

Photo analysis and service providers

Photos are sent through authenticated Firebase Cloud Functions. The backend decodes images in memory, strips metadata, and sends the processed image to OpenAI along with relevant allergies, sensitivities, avoidance and diet choices, goals, age, concern level, and output language. Selected personal details can be sensitive. The analysis request may use a product web search; its instructions restrict search queries to product information and exclude personal profile details.

The analysis path does not upload original photos to Firebase Storage. Saved history contains reports and illustrative thumbnails. Requests to OpenAI use store: false; this does not establish zero provider retention. Provider retention and data controls still need confirmation.

Firebase supports authentication, database storage, server processing, App Check, and analytics. RevenueCat and Apple StoreKit handle purchase validation and entitlements. Apple DeviceCheck supports referral device-eligibility checks. Network-derived rate-limit records and app/device verification help prevent abuse.

Analytics behavior

The current native app enables Firebase Analytics automatically on startup, without an opt-in dialog. It reports allowlisted usage events such as screen views, onboarding progress, scan outcomes, and purchase interactions. Firebase Analytics also uses installation, device, and session information. The driver denies advertising storage, advertising user data, and ad personalization.

Older documentation describes optional consent and withdrawal, but that does not match the current controller. The owner must resolve the intended consent and withdrawal behavior before this policy is finalized.

Retention and deletion

The source schedules personal profiles and scan reports for cleanup after 30 days of inactivity. Initialization, profile saves, and accepted scans renew activity. Cleanup runs hourly in batches, can defer around active work, and is also checked at account initialization; this is not a guarantee of deletion at an exact deadline.

Account deletion removes the Firebase Auth user, personal profile, scan reports, and attempt metadata. The client then recovers an empty account under the retained identity. Billing and usage ledgers, recovery mappings, and referral antifraud records are outside that personal-data cleanup. The referral implementation retains eligibility and redemption records and uses persistent Apple device state to prevent repeated rewards. The reviewed implementation does not establish a finite retention period for these remaining records.

No duration is asserted here for provider records, analytics, logs, backups, or local image caches. Their retention and deletion controls require confirmation.

Contact and data requests

For privacy questions or help accessing, correcting, or deleting data, email gaphbahe@gmail.com. Account deletion is available in the app’s Settings. Never email your recovery key. Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Owner confirmation required before publishing

  • Identify the responsible legal operator and required contact details; the portfolio author’s name is not a confirmed legal identity.
  • Confirm the released app’s data flows, analytics consent and withdrawal controls, applicable legal grounds, and handling of sensitive and family/child profile data.
  • Define justified retention and deletion/export handling for billing, recovery, referral and device records, analytics, logs, backups, and local caches; verify the deployed cleanup job.
  • Confirm provider arrangements, processing locations, international-transfer safeguards, OpenAI account data controls, and actual sharing practices. No blanket no-sharing or no-training assurance is made.
  • Confirm applicable user rights and request procedures, intended age/audience rules, and an effective date; align the app disclosures and App Store privacy labels.

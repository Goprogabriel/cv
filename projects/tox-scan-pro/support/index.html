TOX SCAN PRO / HELP

Support

Help with scans, purchases, and your account.

Contact

Email gaphbahe@gmail.com. Include your app version, iPhone model, iOS version, what happened, the error message, and the approximate time. Remove personal information from screenshots. Never send your recovery key, password, or payment card details.

Restore purchases

Use the Apple Account that made the purchase. Open the app’s subscription screen and tap Restore purchases. Check your connection and allow the app to synchronize. If access or a scan-pack balance is still missing, contact support with the product name and purchase date. Do not repurchase simply to troubleshoot.

Manage or cancel a subscription

On your iPhone, open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Tox Scan Pro to view or manage your subscription. Deleting the app or its account does not cancel an Apple subscription.

Monthly, three-month, and annual Plus plans each include 50 scans per UTC calendar month; the allowance follows the UTC calendar rather than the subscription’s billing anniversary. Additional scan packs are available. Current prices appear in the app.

Scanning troubleshooting

  • Check your internet connection and remaining scans, and install the latest available app update.
  • Use a sharp, well-lit photo. Keep the complete ingredient label readable and avoid glare. For meals, include a clear view of the food.
  • If the camera or photo library is unavailable, check Tox Scan Pro’s permissions in iPhone Settings. Try the other input method.
  • Check the selected family profile and its preferences if a result seems unexpected.
  • If a scan fails, note the error and time. Contact support if it persists or if you believe scans were deducted incorrectly.

Assessments are informational, not medical advice or guarantees of food safety. An uncertain result needs verification with the label or preparer; a photo cannot establish allergen-free status.

Account deletion and data help

Account deletion is available in the app’s Settings. Read the confirmation before proceeding: personal profiles and saved results are deleted, while some usage, payment, recovery, and referral antifraud records remain. If you cannot access the app, email support to request account-deletion help or ask about access, correction, or deletion of your data. Do not email your recovery key.

Manage any Apple subscription separately before removing the app. The linked privacy draft explains the implementation and the retention decisions that still require owner confirmation.

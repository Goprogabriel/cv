What we process and why

InformationUse and storage
Voice recordingsThe microphone starts only after you shake or tap and grant permission. Recording is visibly indicated, ends after at most 20 seconds, and is cancelled when you leave the app. With your consent, a recording is sent via the sayso server to OpenAI for transcription. The device's temporary clip is deleted after a successful answer or when discarded; a failed request can keep the clip available for your retry. The server temporarily validates audio and removes its temporary audio file after processing. Audio is not kept in the question database. Crashes or operating-system interruptions can delay normal temporary-file cleanup.
Questions, answers and optional memoryTranscribed questions and AI answers are displayed and stored in local history. Your question, mode, language and optional recent theme context are sent to OpenAI to generate a reply. Opting into theme memory keeps up to 10 recent question-and-answer pairs on the device and includes them with new questions in that theme. Stopping memory or changing theme clears that context without clearing your history.
Identity, balances and referralsFirebase creates a pseudonymous app-user identifier without requiring a name or email login. The server uses identifiers, usage counts, question balances, request IDs and hashed request fingerprints to deliver answers, prevent duplicate charges, limit abuse and allocate referral rewards. Native credentials use platform secure storage. These records can still be personal data; they are not treated as irreversibly anonymous.
PurchasesApple handles payments. RevenueCat processes a pseudonymous Firebase-based customer ID, purchase and transaction records, subscription status, and related app/device information to verify purchases, restore subscriptions and process refunds. sayso keeps transaction ownership, balance, usage and deduplication records. We do not receive payment-card details. Bought question packs do not expire; cancelling a subscription does not remove a pack balance.
Technical data and preferencesThe service and infrastructure receive connection information, including IP addresses and operational/security information. Rate limiting uses keyed hashes and counters. Device settings include language, consent, onboarding, sound and haptics preferences. Motion readings are used locally for shake detection. The app does not request GPS location, run advertising, or use cross-app advertising tracking.
Support messagesIf you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send so we can answer and handle your request. Recordings and history are not automatically attached to support emails.

Service providers and international processing

We use Google Firebase and Google Cloud for authentication, database and hosting; OpenAI for voice transcription and AI answers; RevenueCat for purchase administration; and Apple for payments and platform services. The configured sayso database is in an EU Firestore region and the backend is hosted in Google Cloud's europe-west1 region. Providers may process or access information in other countries, including the United States, under their applicable terms, data-processing agreements and transfer safeguards where required.

Answer requests to OpenAI set response storage to false. This does not mean every provider system has zero retention. OpenAI describes default API data-use and retention controls, including endpoint-specific abuse-monitoring retention. Provider records remain subject to the applicable provider controls and any legal obligations. See OpenAI API data controls, Firebase privacy, RevenueCat privacy, and Apple privacy.

How long information remains

Local history and preferences remain until you clear them or the operating system removes the app's data. Device backups can follow your own backup settings. The server makes question-and-answer retry copies available for up to 24 hours; expired text is removed by server cleanup rather than being a permanent history feature. Clearing history requests deletion of the current user's cached text immediately. Operational interruptions can delay scheduled cleanup.

Identity, balances, referral records, transaction ownership, billing events and technical request records have no fixed automatic expiry in the current service. They are retained as needed to preserve purchased questions, administer the service, prevent duplicate credits and fraud, resolve disputes and meet applicable legal obligations. Ask us for deletion or information about records associated with your app identity. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and any associated legal requirements. Infrastructure logs, backups and provider records can have separate provider retention; deleting local history does not automatically delete all such records.

Your controls and rights

  • Change microphone permission in device Settings. Switch off AI processing in Profile to stop new uploads. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing already completed.
  • Delete individual local history entries or use Clear history in Profile to clear local history and request deletion of the server retry cache. Individual local deletion can leave a retry copy until its normal expiry. Clearing history does not reset usage or remove every billing or technical record.
  • Stop or reset theme memory to stop sending that optional context. Demo recordings stay on the device and use marked example answers.
  • For access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection or portability where applicable, contact us. We may need proportionate information to locate and verify your app identity. Do not email your secret session token, password or payment-card details. Some transaction or legal records may need to be retained; we will explain relevant limitations.

For users in the EEA, processing relies, as applicable, on your consent for voice/optional AI context; performance of the requested service and purchase administration; legitimate interests in security, fraud prevention and reliable balances; and compliance with legal obligations. Where consent is the basis, it can be withdrawn. You may complain to your local supervisory authority, including Denmark's Datatilsynet.

Security, age and changes

Release connections use HTTPS. Local text history does not have additional app-specific encryption; device security and backups also affect its protection. No security measure is an absolute guarantee. sayso is not designed for children under 13 and does not verify age. If a child has provided personal information, contact us. This page's effective date identifies the current notice; material service changes will be reflected here and, where appropriate, in the app's information and consent flow.

Optagelser, spørgsmål og svar

Mikrofonen starter først, når du vælger at ryste eller trykke og har givet tilladelse. Optagelsen vises tydeligt, varer højst 20 sekunder og afbrydes, når du forlader appen. Med dit samtykke sendes optagelsen via sayso-serveren til OpenAI til transskription og et svar. Den midlertidige fil på telefonen slettes efter et vellykket svar, eller når du kasserer den. Ved fejl kan den være tilgængelig til dit eget genforsøg. Serverens midlertidige lydfil fjernes efter behandlingen; lyd gemmes ikke i spørgsmålsdatabasen. Nedbrud kan forsinke almindelig oprydning.

Spørgsmål og svar gemmes i din lokale historik. Tilvalgt temahukommelse gemmer op til 10 seneste samtaler på telefonen og sender dem med som kontekst ved nye spørgsmål i samme tema. Stop eller temaskift rydder denne kontekst uden at slette historikken. Demo bruger tydeligt markerede eksempler, og demooptagelser sendes ikke til AI.

Identitet, køb og tekniske oplysninger

Firebase opretter en pseudonym brugeridentitet uden krav om navn eller e-mail-login. Identifikatorer, forbrug, saldi, invitationsbonus, forespørgsels-id'er og hashede fingeraftryk bruges til at levere svar, undgå dobbeltbetaling og begrænse misbrug. Apple håndterer betaling. RevenueCat modtager en Firebase-baseret kundeidentitet, købs- og transaktionsoplysninger, abonnementsstatus og relevante app-/enhedsoplysninger. sayso gemmer blandt andet ejerskab af transaktioner, forbrug og hændelser, så spørgsmål ikke krediteres to gange. Vi modtager ikke kortoplysninger.

Server og infrastruktur modtager forbindelsesoplysninger, herunder IP-adresse og drifts-/sikkerhedsoplysninger. Begrænsning af forespørgsler bruger nøglebaserede hashes og tællere. Telefonen gemmer indstillinger som sprog, samtykke, lyd og vibration. Bevægelsesdata bruges lokalt til at registrere ryst. Appen anmoder ikke om GPS-position, viser ikke annoncer og bruger ikke sporing på tværs af apps til reklamer.

Privacy policy

Updated 11 November 2026

ParkRead is provided by Gabriel Back. Contact us at gaphbahe@gmail.com about privacy or support.

What we collect

ParkRead creates an anonymous account identifier to associate scans, vehicles and purchases with your account. When you scan, we process the photos you submit, the selected parking location, parking time, language and optional vehicle and permit details. Photos may contain people or vehicle registration numbers. Please photograph only what is needed.

You may choose a location on a map or allow foreground location access. Location information is stored with the scan. Vehicle details can include a disability parking permit setting, which may reveal sensitive information. Providing it is optional. We also maintain scan results, credit balances, referral records, purchase references and technical security and service logs.

How information is used and shared

We use this information to provide and save scan results, apply relevant parking context, manage purchases and credits, support users, and protect the service. Google Firebase and Google Cloud provide authentication, database, storage and server services. After you agree to the in-app notice, our server sends your photos and relevant city, time and vehicle context to OpenAI to interpret the sign. Your account ID, saved registration number and precise coordinates are not sent as separate fields to OpenAI, but photos may contain identifying details.

Apple processes App Store payments. RevenueCat helps manage purchase records and subscription events. We do not receive full payment card details. Service providers may process data outside your country under their applicable terms and safeguards. We do not use scan data for targeted advertising.

Retention and deletion

Scans and their uploaded photos are kept for your history until you delete the scan in the app or request deletion. To delete one, open History, select the scan and choose Delete scan. Related copies may persist temporarily in backups or service logs. Purchase and security records may need to be retained for legitimate legal, accounting or fraud-prevention purposes. Account deletion is not currently offered as an in-app option; contact us for a privacy request. Deleting the app or a scan does not cancel an Apple subscription; manage subscriptions in your Apple account settings.

Your choices and rights

You can decline location permission and select a place manually, decline AI processing and remove individual scans. Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict or obtain a copy of your personal information, and to object or withdraw consent where applicable. Contact gaphbahe@gmail.com; we may need information to verify the request. You may also contact your data protection authority, including Datatilsynet in Denmark.

Security and changes

We use access controls and encrypted connections to protect the service, but no system can guarantee absolute security. We may update this policy as the app changes. The current version will be posted here.

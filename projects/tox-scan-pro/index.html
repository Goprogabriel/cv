Tox Scan Pro

A clearer look at what’s on your plate.

Use AI to analyze photos of meals and ingredient labels, highlighting possible ingredients, allergens, nutritional concerns, and uncertainty.

App support →

Personal to you. Useful for the family.

Your food profile

Personalize assessments around allergies, sensitivities, ingredients you avoid, and food preferences. Keep separate profiles for family members.

From photo to report

Take a photo or choose one from your photo library. Revisit saved reports and keep favorites close at hand.

Made for everyday use

Multilingual support and referral rewards complement the scanning experience.

Scans and Plus

Start with three welcome scans. Monthly, three-month, and annual Plus subscriptions each include 50 scans per UTC calendar month. Additional scan packs are available. See the app’s purchase screen for current prices and purchase details.

Information with limits

Food assessments are informational, not medical advice or guarantees of food safety. AI can miss ingredients and allergens. Verify the physical label or ask the person preparing the meal, including about cross-contamination.

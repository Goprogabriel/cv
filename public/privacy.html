Privacy policy

Updated 6 November 2026

Account & sharing

Firebase Authentication handles Apple, Google and email sign-in. Apple lets you hide your email address. Firestore stores your profile, approved connections, private groups and the audience for each shared moment. Storage holds compressed photographs. Only the audience saved for a moment can access its gallery.

Photos & permissions

The camera runs when you open a capture invitation and enable it. You explicitly choose Ready before capture. Saving photographs requests permission to add them to your photo library. Unsent captures stay in a private local outbox for retry.

Analytics

Usage analytics are optional and off by default. Defined events do not contain photographs, names, emails or invitation codes. Your analytics choice is stored on this device.

Deletion & reports

Deleting an account immediately blocks access, then removes its profile and uploaded photographs in the background. Previously saved copies on other devices cannot be recalled. Reports are stored privately for review.

Storage & retention

Account details and shared content are stored while needed to provide your account and galleries. Deleting your account starts background removal of your profile, authentication account and uploaded photographs. Other participants’ photographs and shared-moment records can remain in their original audience’s history. Security records can remain to prevent access with old tokens. Contact support for questions about your information.

Service providers

Google Firebase provides authentication, database, photo storage and backend processing. If you enable usage analytics, Google Analytics processes interaction events, app-instance identifiers, device information and approximate location derived from network information. The app does not request GPS location or use analytics for advertising. Expo and Apple process notification delivery data when you enable moment notifications.

Roam Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 9, 2026

Roam is a currency converter provided by Gabriel Hesthaven. This policy explains how the app handles information.

Location

If you choose the location feature and grant permission, Roam accesses your device's location while the app is in use to identify your country and suggest its currency. The operating system's reverse-geocoding service is used to identify the country. Roam does not store your coordinates or send them to the exchange-rate provider. You can decline access or change your permission in iOS Settings.

Camera and photos

If you use the price scanner, you can grant camera access or choose a photo. Price recognition is performed on your device. Images used for scanning are not uploaded by Roam and temporary scan files are deleted after processing.

Exchange rates and local storage

Roam requests exchange rates and currency information from Frankfurter (api.frankfurter.dev). As with any internet request, that service and its infrastructure receive network information such as your IP address. The app stores settings, favorites and cached exchange rates on your device to support your choices and offline use.

Reviews

Roam may request an App Store rating using Apple's review prompt. Apple determines whether to show it and handles any rating or review you submit.

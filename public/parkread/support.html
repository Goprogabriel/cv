How can we help?

Questions about a scan, purchase or privacy? Contact ParkRead support and tell us what happened. Please don't include sensitive photos or payment details unless needed.

Email support

Scanning a sign

Take or choose one clear photo; you can add up to two more. Select your parking location and start the scan. Results are guidance only: always check the signs and markings at the location.

Purchases and subscriptions

Additional scans and the monthly plan are purchased through Apple. Prices appear before you confirm. Manage or cancel a subscription in your Apple account settings. Apple handles payment and refund requests.

Delete a scan

Open History, select the scan and choose Delete scan. For privacy requests or help with your account, contact us by email.
